provenance-gate

A SLSA provenance verifier that checks signed attestations against policy, then rebuilds the artifact and diffs it to show the binary came from the reviewed source rather than trusting the attestation alone.

  • Rejects with 9 specific reason codes, including DIGEST_MISMATCH, REPLAYED and NOT_REPRODUCIBLE
  • 19 tests covering the genuine path and each tamper class
  • Python standard library only, with a browser demo of the decision workflow
