cannibal

Search cannibalisation detection that tests every within-query page pair for genuine competition and corrects for multiple testing. The output is a short list of real competing pairs instead of hundreds of noisy ones.

  • One site: 173 uncorrected flags (161 false) against 14 with Benjamini-Hochberg (2 false)
  • 3,575 page pairs tested; all 12 planted pairs found
  • Pure standard-library Python, with a seeded generator that measures precision
  • Python
  • Chi-square test
  • Benjamini-Hochberg
  • Bonferroni
  • unittest
